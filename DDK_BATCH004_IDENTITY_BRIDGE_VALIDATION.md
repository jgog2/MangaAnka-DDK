### Existing bridge and missing contract

The shared publication_specials.match_special() already supports exact provider/source/reader-ID/original-label aliases to independently trusted canonical numeric specials. The DDK uses that function through real normalization and manifest projection, retaining the original acquisition record. That mechanism is preferable to a competing resolver. However, current publisher supplementary observations deliberately have canonical_identifier=None and acquisition_binding=unresolved. The canonical special lists, ordering, missing-acquisition sets and several consumers assume numeric Decimal keys. Supplying isbn:toc:page as a chapter key or assigning a convenient provider fraction would violate those contracts.

No automatic supplementary bridge was added. A compatible future contract needs an opaque canonical supplementary identity namespace separate from chapter numbering, exact canonical work/edition/language, immutable source publication ID (ISBN/JDCN plus typed content record), an explicit publisher relation or reviewed content-equivalence proof to an exact provider reader ID and original label, and separate identity-recognition versus standard-volume-membership authority. Preserve raw acquisition rows and contrary evidence; ambiguous main/special or competing bindings must fail closed. Do not make scan OCR, title similarity, numerical proximity or page count alone authoritative. Collection page positions locate evidence, not chapter IDs. This requires a deliberate shared publication-model extension, rather than a small safe patch in this pass.
