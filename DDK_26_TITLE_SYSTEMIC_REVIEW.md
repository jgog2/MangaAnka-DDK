## Shared identity correction

The root cause is in the local plugin's `enrichment_matching.py`. `match_external_identity` could call an alias-only match with the same author HIGH. `enrich_content_results` then collected **every** HIGH external candidate under a normalized provider title family and joined their IDs into one `canonical_work_id`, without requiring a unique ID per trusted service or checking serial versus one-shot format and reported totals. `canonical_identity.py` grouping and `effective_search_rows` then carried that compound identity into selection and `reference_integration.py` publication context.

The approved plugin correction now demotes alias-only textual matches, rejects explicit standard/colored mismatches, and permits at most one selected identity per external service. A direct provider external ID or unique cross-service corroboration can resolve a same-service conflict. Cross-service corroboration requires a shared explicit external ID, or a shared core title plus matching creators or both reported chapter and volume totals, without an explicit contradiction. Title overlap alone is insufficient. An unresolved family gets `_canonical_identity_confidence='ambiguous'` and no trusted canonical work ID or inherited creator/volume facts. The two former expected-failure DDK tests now pass. A 144-chapter/14-volume offline publication control confirms that the clean main identity remains fully mapped. No live 26-title rerun was done.

A final DDK edition guard uses the selected provider's own title, badge, color flag, and explicit edition. Aliases do not trigger color classification. An explicitly fan-colored selected row blocks a standard request with `WRONG_EDITION_MATCH` and no downstream mapping recommendations.

The DDK now computes `structural_coverage` separately from source parse validity. It records final mapped/logical counts and volume coverage, plus each MangaDex/Wikipedia source's mapped acquisition keys and volume count. Less than one-quarter chapter or unambiguous reported-volume coverage is sparse; an incomplete result above that is partial. Reported volume totals are diagnostic only, and conflicting totals are not used as an expectation. The existing plugin reference service already attempts MangaDex and Wikipedia; its manifest lets Wikipedia fill unmapped gaps after a sparse valid MangaDex result. If neither supplies enough structure, `UNMAPPED_CHAPTERS` and publication REVIEW remain. No boundaries are invented and no new reference source is added.

Repeated fractional records with titles such as `Mission 37.5` remain `provider_fractional_candidate` with candidate confidence. They do not become canonical main chapters or recognized specials without independent evidence. Split candidates remain candidates; range bundles retain syntax confidence. Aggregation and all member provenance remain available.
